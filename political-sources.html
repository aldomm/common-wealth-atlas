Government follows place and year

The government panel updates from the selected reference place or historical source entity and selected year. It never retains an unrelated manual government selection. Annual source records are labeled as annual; they do not claim to describe the exact map day.

Matching policy

Reference places use corresponding source-country identifiers, with explicit Kosovo/Somaliland and legacy-code aliases. Historical entities use a saved source-label association table: exact source polity names, explicit historical aliases, or an explicit governmental prefix followed by a uniquely matching source country name. Every association is bounded by the historical source's year interval and the requested source year must exist. There is no fuzzy matching, nearest-year leader substitution or geometry-based assertion of sovereignty.

The table currently associates 502 source labels across CShapes world, CShapes Europe and Cliopatria; 376 source labels remain unmapped. This includes ambiguous empires, federations and factions. These counts refer to source labels, not unique countries. The method is a governing-polity series association, not a fully verified territorial crosswalk. For example, Russia/USSR uses V-Dem's longitudinal Russia series with its own dated regime description; former-USSR aggregate statistics do not automatically receive a single government. Republic of China is not joined to the mainland series after 1949. Colonial-era source units may describe governors or colonial executives.

Audit and reproducibility: research/build_government_links.py, government-link-audit.json, and test_governments.cjs. Missing mappings are explicitly unavailable; no government type, party, leader or ideology is inferred from population, wealth or geography.

Definitions

Government type, executive title, party affiliation and ideological orientation are distinct. The Bjørnskov–Rode institutional categories are displayed separately from V-Dem's electoral/liberal regime typology. Provider differences are not silently reconciled. A monarchy indicator does not establish an executive monarch's powers. Party affiliation describes an executive's party, not the entire governing coalition.

Heads of Government's Other category includes both positions outside its three main categories and insufficient/conflicting evidence; it is not converted to centrism. Its religious flag concerns an explicitly Christian political platform, not a leader's personal faith. The downloaded CSV decodes as UTF-8 despite the codebook's general Latin-1 note; names are preserved. Date strings use month/day/year as confirmed by unambiguous dates. Open end dates are capped by the dataset's 2023 coverage and never projected forward.

V-Dem ideology-character scores describe nationalism, socialist/communist, restorative/conservative, separatist/autonomist and religious legitimation claims. All available mean scores are retained without a majority threshold; categories can coexist. These are not an ideological composition of government. Full cabinet composition, complete daily executive histories and exhaustive constitutional rules remain absent.
