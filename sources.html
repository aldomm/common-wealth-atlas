Source register

Data: Maddison Project Database 2023, Jutta Bolt and Jan Luiten van Zanden. Cite Bolt & van Zanden (2024), “Maddison style estimates of the evolution of the world economy: A new 2023 update”, Journal of Economic Surveys. Paper · Dataset (CC BY 4.0).

The bibliography below reproduces country source listings from the workbook. Date ranges refer to source coverage, not independent validation of each displayed row. Early entries are reconstructions; source interpolation is not separately flagged by this extract. GDP/person is not wages, wealth, household income or happiness. Country labels identify source statistical series, not 1820 sovereign states. Historical territorial definitions need country-by-country review.

Geography: Natural Earth, public domain, 1:110m physical land polygons. Project interpolation uses linear values between supported endpoints at most eight years apart in allowlisted series periods only; three Portuguese GDP/person snapshots (1856, 1860, 1864) are estimated. The popup gives both endpoint years and values. No extrapolation. Modern Natural Earth admin-0 reference outlines are displayed; these are not historical sovereignty or borders. Geography file retrieved 13 November 2026; the download was not release-tagged. Source file hash is retained with the built data. Modern label points and approximate manual anchors locate statistical series only. Do not sum overlapping former/modern units.
