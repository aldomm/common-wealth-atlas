READ BEFORE INTERPRETING

A map for asking better questions

Every quantity is invented in normalized model units (illustrative units). No country ranking, wealth estimate or institutional effect is a historical measurement. Regions are illustrative aggregates: Britain excludes Ireland here; German lands and Iberia are not single 1820 states. Shapes are hand-drawn.

Stocks, flows and double counting

Bubble area ∝ household assets minus debt. All household values are group totals, not per-person estimates. Company equity is already part of owners’ assets; never add it again to personal wealth. Annual output, wages and saving are flows. Stock and flow snapshots are independently illustrative; savings do not drive future wealth. Negative net wealth would have no filled area.

Visual demonstration, not simulation

Time interpolates invented display values. No causal economic engine or policy effect is implemented. Future rules should let us test, rather than assume, the roles of competition, worker protections, institutions, initial wealth, innovation and luck. Public, private, cooperative and nonprofit provision belong in that later design.

Trade arrows are illustrative goods relationships, not measured volumes. Income transfers sum to zero across the four regions, but this is not a stock-flow-consistent economy. Colonial extraction, enslaved labor and external suppliers are critical omissions; Europe was not self-contained.

Evidence and next steps

OECD: How Was Life? (2014) motivates separate dimensions of well-being rather than GDP alone.

OECD: historical extreme poverty (2021) uses reconstructed data and geographic conventions; uncertainty is substantial.

World Bank: Measuring Poverty explains modern PPP-based lines. Do not splice these onto historical estimates as a seamless series.

UK Parliament: regulation and trade documents industry’s regulatory setting and dependence on external markets and inputs.

Next: documented borders, sector accounts, ownership matrices, household weights, resource and chokepoint power, innovation entrants, stochastic mobility, health, education and hours. Governments and institutions long predate 1820. The French Revolution, unions and Marxist critiques require dedicated sourced context.
