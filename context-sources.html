India sanitation programme · study window

2014–2020 · local context

Swachh Bharat Mission began in 2014. A 2024 district-level study examined sanitation and child mortality using 2011–2020 data. The authors estimated associations with lower infant and under-five mortality; this is not an atlas estimate of life-expectancy or fiscal gains.

2014–2020 is the study’s post-launch observation window, not the programme’s end. Local construction and uptake varied.

Chakrabarti and colleagues · Scientific Reports (2024)

COVID-19 international emergency

Emergency-designation window, not the full period of infections or economic effects.
