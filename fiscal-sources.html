Debt, prices and policy · sources

Seven distinct measures. Public debt is a stock; revenue, expenditure, primary balance and interest costs are annual flows. All five are percentages of GDP, not dollar amounts. Primary balance excludes interest expenditure, and interest cost excludes principal repayments.

CPI inflation measures annual changes in local consumer prices. Policy interest rates are December end-of-period observations, not annual averages, lending rates or inflation-adjusted interest rates.

These ratios are not summed or averaged for country groups. Individual country series remain visible. Missing years are not interpolated, and older map records retain their source dates. Official compilations can contain estimates and reconstructions.

IMF fiscal coverage can be general or central government and change through time. Historical reference areas do not automatically match the atlas historical borders. The API does not expose every underlying coverage flag. Consult provider documentation before interpreting breaks.
