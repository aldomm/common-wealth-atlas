Life expectancy at birth

Life expectancy at birth indicates the number of years a newborn infant would live if prevailing patterns of mortality at the time of its birth were to stay the same throughout its life.

At least basic drinking water

The percentage of people using at least basic water services. This indicator encompasses both people using basic water services as well as those using safely managed water services. Basic drinking water services is defined as drinking water from an improved source, provided collection time is not more than 30 minutes for a round trip. Improved water sources include piped water, boreholes or tubewells, protected dug wells, protected springs, and packaged or delivered water.

Unit: % of population. WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP) for Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene, World Health Organization (WHO), uri: washdata.org, publisher: WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP) for Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene (washdata.org), date accessed: 2025-09-30, date published: 2025-08-25; UN Children's Fund (UNICEF), uri: washdata.org, note: WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP) for Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene

At least basic sanitation

The percentage of people using at least basic sanitation services, that is, improved sanitation facilities that are not shared with other households. This indicator encompasses both people using basic sanitation services as well as those using safely managed sanitation services. Improved sanitation facilities include flush/pour flush to piped sewer systems, septic tanks or pit latrines; ventilated improved pit latrines, composting toilets or pit latrines with slabs.

Unit: % of population. WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP) for Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene, World Health Organization (WHO), uri: washdata.org, publisher: WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP) for Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene (washdata.org), date accessed: 2025-09-30, date published: 2025-08-25; UN Children's Fund (UNICEF), uri: washdata.org, note: WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP) for Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene

Crude birth rate

Crude birth rate indicates the number of live births occurring during the year, per 1,000 population estimated at midyear. Subtracting the crude death rate from the crude birth rate provides the rate of natural increase, which is equal to the rate of population change in the absence of migration.

Unit: births per 1,000 people/year. World Population Prospects, United Nations (UN), publisher: UN Population Division; Statistical databases and publications from national statistical offices, National Statistical Offices (NSOs); Demographic Statistics, Eurostat (ESTAT); Population and Vital Statistics Report (various years), United Nations (UN), publisher: UN Statistical Division

Exports of goods and services

Exports of goods includes changes in the economic ownership of goods from residents of the compiling economy to non-residents, irrespective of physical movement of goods across national borders. Exports of services includes services provided by residents to non-residents. This indicator is expressed as a percentage of Gross Domestic Product (GDP) which is the total income earned through the production of goods and services in an economic territory during an accounting period.

Unit: % of GDP. Country official statistics, National Statistical Offices (NSOs); National Accounts data files, Central Banks; Staff estimates, World Bank (WB)

Production and sector shares

35,025 WDI observations, available years 1960–2025; coverage varies by country and sector. Value added as a percentage of GDP. Manufacturing is included in industry, not an additional component. Broad shares are not normalized to 100% and should not be multiplied by reconstructed PPP GDP without reconciling definitions.

  • Agriculture, forestry and fishing: 9,088 observations, 206 economies
  • Industry including construction: 9,055 observations, 208 economies
  • Manufacturing: 8,253 observations, 204 economies
  • Services: 8,629 observations, 205 economies

World Bank WDI and original providers in each indicator metadata. Downloaded 13 November 2026. WDI default CC BY 4.0 terms. No atlas interpolation for these shares; missing years show the last dated record where available.

Historical sector reconstructions

3,590 annual records for France, Netherlands and United Kingdom, within the atlas timeline from 1820. France and Netherlands shares are calculated from the same-year current-price sector value added and total GDP in each original workbook. UK series are published volume indexes with 1913 = 100, never sector shares. Gaps are preserved, including wartime gaps; no interpolation or modern-sector splicing.

Source: Smits, Woltjer and Ma (2009), A Dataset on Comparative Historical National Accounts, ca. 1870–1950: A Time-Series Perspective, GGDC Research Memorandum GD-107. Original country authors and source workbooks remain linked from each indicator. Provider data and attribution requirement. Source permits use for any purpose with attribution. Historical territorial scope follows each reconstruction; modern map outlines are reference locations only.
