Trade balances: goods and services

Return to the Atlas

Calculated balance = exports − imports. A positive result is a surplus; a negative result is a deficit. Values are percentages of GDP, so they require no currency or inflation conversion.

The Atlas subtracts only matched annual country observations with the same WDI vintage, geographic label and GDP units. Input shares are from the World Bank release updated 11 November 2026 and retrieved 13 November 2026. Missing inputs are not zero and are not interpolated. The source rows and both input values remain in the evidence.

This is an Atlas calculation from published data, not a separately downloaded reported balance. Source figures can themselves contain official or staff estimates. Country reference areas may differ from historical sovereignty.

What this is not

The current account also includes primary income and transfers. Merchandise-only partner links and product totals have a narrower scope. We do not add incomplete partner records to fabricate a national balance or convert a % of GDP balance to dollars by multiplying it by purchasing-power GDP.

A surplus is not inherently good, nor a deficit inherently bad. The color scale therefore shows low to high, not economic success or failure. Group comparisons show country ratios separately, since adding ratios would be meaningless and weighting them requires compatible nominal GDP denominators.
